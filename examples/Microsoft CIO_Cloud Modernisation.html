## 6. Meeting Strategy

### Desired Meeting Outcome

Secure CIO sponsorship to pilot or expand Microsoft’s latest AI and cloud capabilities into new business areas tied to measurable business outcomes.

### Recommended Conversation Flow

1. Align on current business goals and top digital initiatives
2. Share Microsoft benchmarks on AI/cloud-driven impact
3. Identify specific modernization/automation priorities
4. Explore expansion opportunities in untapped functions or markets
5. Agree on next steps, success metrics, and executive sponsorship

### Topics to Explore

- Impact of recent AI-driven innovations on business outcomes
- Time-to-value and operational impact of current Microsoft solutions
- Areas where cloud modernization can unlock competitive advantage
- Stakeholder alignment and readiness for expansion

### Topics to Avoid

- Detailed technical architecture discussions
- Retrospective reviews of legacy deployments
- Overly broad transformation concepts without actionable next steps

### Risks to Watch For

- Perceived complexity or risk of additional modernization
- Lack of clear business outcome linkage for new investments
- Change fatigue within impacted business units

### Signals That Suggest Expansion Opportunities

- CIO expresses urgency to scale successful pilots
- Business units proactively request new features or solutions
- Positive NPS or business outcome metrics from recent deployments
