## 6. Meeting Strategy

### Desired Meeting Outcome

Secure renewal by clearly showing how extending regulatory support enables ambitious AI expansion while minimizing compliance risks.

### Recommended Conversation Flow

1. Recap JPMorgan’s AI-driven financial/operational gains.
2. Discuss alignment between our regulatory capabilities and their compliance needs.
3. Identify pain points in scaling AI adoption within regulatory frameworks.
4. Explore co-innovation opportunities for further efficiency and risk reduction.
5. Align on success metrics and next steps.

### Topics to Explore

- How regulatory hurdles are impacting AI/cloud expansion
- Successes and current blockers in scaling AI projects
- Required capabilities for the next wave of AI use cases
- Opportunities to automate and streamline compliance efforts

### Topics to Avoid

- Generic technology roadmaps without business context
- Overly detailed product features not directly tied to outcomes
- Broad AI trends with no JPMorgan linkage

### Risks to Watch For

- Stakeholder skepticism about ROI of compliance solutions
- Concerns about disruption to existing AI workflows
- Unclear ownership of compliance outcomes in AI expansion

### Signals That Suggest Expansion Opportunities

- Mention of new AI initiatives moving into regulated domains
- Frustration over existing compliance bottlenecks
- Interest in measurable efficiency improvements from current partnership

## 7. Discovery Questions

1. Where have you seen the most business value generated from current AI and cloud investments—and how is regulatory compliance supporting or hindering scale?
2. What challenges are you experiencing as you look to extend AI adoption into new or more regulated areas of the business?
3. How are you measuring success for AI-driven transformation, both in terms of operational efficiency and compliance risk?
4. In your view, what compliance hurdles would need to be removed or streamlined to accelerate the next phase of AI-driven growth?
5. Which additional business units or processes are you considering bringing into the AI transformation journey in the upcoming year, and what support would be most valuable to ensure their success?
