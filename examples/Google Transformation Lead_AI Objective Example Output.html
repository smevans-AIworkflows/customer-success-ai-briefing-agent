## 6. Meeting Strategy

### Desired Meeting Outcome

Align with the Head of Transformation on key AI business outcomes, secure buy-in for targeted transformation pilots, and identify tangible metrics for success measurement.

### Recommended Conversation Flow

1. Confirm Google’s current business objectives and AI transformation progress.
2. Discuss AI-driven opportunities highlighted in recent financial results.
3. Explore highest-impact adoption areas and potential blockers.
4. Identify required stakeholders and agree on next steps.

### Topics to Explore

- Measurable AI-driven business outcomes
- Barriers to scaling AI initiatives
- Integration across platforms (Gemini, Workspace)
- Organizational alignment and change management

### Topics to Avoid

- Deep technical solution architecture
- Early-stage “blue sky” ideas without direct business impact evidence

### Risks to Watch For

- Overestimating AI adoption speed or readiness
- Underaddressing integration and change barriers

### Signals That Suggest Expansion Opportunities

- Strong pilot results in discrete business units
- Interest in cross-functional AI standardization
- Leadership appetite for external AI collaborations
